Advanced Metal Research

Learning and Autonomy Engineer (Hawthorne, July 2026 to present). I build research software across the robotic-welding stack: the shared run-data platform from robot controller to queryable evidence, weld and materials models, motion research, multi-factor optimization, evaluation, and process-off integration, and I lead three to five of the team’s interns.

I defined typed models for weld books, materials, joints, process windows, studies, trajectories, metallurgy outputs, traces, and evidence, with randomized experiments and content-addressed capture of human and simulator trajectories. Torch orientation and robot/positioner coordination are optimization variables rather than fixed constraints, and independent kinematic, collision, calibration, continuity, and process checks stay authoritative over execution.

Run evidence is bound to source revision, binaries, robot model, calibration, collision assets, and request/response pairs, so a planning result can be re-derived rather than trusted. I also designed the staged path from simulation to supervised process-off trials, and built Steam Deck operator controls and runtime integration that contributed to operator-confirmed jogging on the assembled robot. Full trajectory execution and welding remain separate validation work.

I also build the software-factory and agent workflows the team uses to develop, test, and review the robotics software, held to the same replayable evidence as the planner.

T-UEBA

Principal investigator on a $250k Army SBIR at RAM Laboratories in tactical user and entity behavior analytics. The model was a constrained heterogeneous temporal graph network over user, resource, and action graphs, with calibrated graph attention, conformal prediction, sparse-autoencoder interpretability, active learning, and curriculum training.

Results: 95% accuracy, 0.887 F1, under 3% false positives, 15.2 ms per graph, and a 15 MB model, 400× smaller and 7.9× faster than the baseline, under shifting data and hard compute limits. In evaluation the system also generated candidate enforcement rules for zero-trust policy decision and enforcement points.

DXF/CAD representation

A work trial on Augrade’s DXF-to-CAD problem: research into reconstructing editable CAD from drawings using geometric objects, relation graphs, and deterministic checks. The proposed learned component repairs relations that geometry alone cannot resolve. A public geometry workbench recovered 2,237 polygons from approximately 67,000 CAD primitives across 111 layers, with parameter sweeps and SVG review artifacts.
