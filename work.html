Epistemology & Natural Philosophy

2017–present

Three essays published on this site explore the same question from different angles: how does perception shape understanding, and how do structures of knowledge constrain what we can discover?

  • “Fiction is All That is Seen” — Plato’s Cave, narrative as translator between reality and cognition
  • “Mindful at Every Step” — Maxwell’s purity of mind, architecture on uncertain foundations
  • “Something that’s Sound” — harmonic structure, Schoenberg, the six core story trajectories
  • “Compression Ecology” — predictive attention as ecological niche between motivation and inspiration

Hamilton taught that causal and analogical thinking are necessitated by the limitations of the brain: all one learns of the world is gained through the senses, liable to distortion; producing knowledge that is relative rather than absolute. The dynamical tradition in physics is propelled from that understanding.

We search for what is architecturally sound. It is in this search that logic and spirit coincide.
