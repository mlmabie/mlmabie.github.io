Member of Technical Staff from April 2022, then Senior Member of Technical Staff and principal investigator from April 2024 to March 2025. Across programs I owned modeling, data pipelines, evaluation, and integration, and wrote more than fifteen SBIR proposals.

Program snapshots

Evaluation
