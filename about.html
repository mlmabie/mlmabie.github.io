About

I started designing tools for medical-device manufacturing at sixteen. I built CAD, fixtures, pneumatic tooling, robotic end effectors, prototypes, and qualification tests beside the production line.

I studied Cognitive Science with a Machine Learning and Neural Computation specialization at UC San Diego. My work included reinforcement learning, neural data science, and design research with Hyundai and Ford.

At RAM Labs I designed systems for tactical cybersecurity and vulnerability repair. I led a $250k Army SBIR as principal investigator and co-invented a patent-filed deep-learning repair architecture.

I now build robot motion planning tools for Advanced Metal Research. The same method runs through the work: represent the object, name the constraints, expose uncertainty, and test the next decision.

Outside engineering, I play and teach piano. Music trained the same habit of close listening and revision.
