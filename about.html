About

I’m a research engineer working on robotics and AI systems. My current work at Advanced Metal Research spans motion planning, operator interfaces, and robot runtime integration.

I began at Applied Medical at sixteen, designing tools for medical-device manufacturing. Over five years, I worked on pneumatic manipulators, fixtures, robotic end-of-arm tooling, prototypes, and production troubleshooting.

Before that, I co-led mechanical design in FIRST Robotics. I also played competitive piano, taught, improvised, and performed.

At UC San Diego I studied Cognitive Science with a specialization in Machine Learning and Neural Computation, alongside reinforcement learning, neural data science, and design research.

At RAM Laboratories, I led a $250k Army SBIR in tactical behavior analytics, worked on automated vulnerability repair and distributed inference, and co-invented a deep-learning bug-fixing patent application.

I now work through Mabie Industries. My interests include model evaluation, calibrated decisions, and the tools people use to inspect and correct AI systems.
