I build robotics software, agent evaluation systems, and production tools. At Advanced Metal Research, my work spans motion-planning evaluation, Steam Deck operator controls, and robot runtime integration. Recent integration work contributed to operator-confirmed jogging on the assembled robot.

Previously, I led research in tactical behavior analytics and worked on automated code repair at RAM Laboratories. I started in manufacturing engineering at Applied Medical and studied cognitive science, machine learning, and neural computation at UC San Diego.

I’ve also shipped six production systems for Drone Institute, from thermal imagery and GIS workflows to customer operations. I own the work from requirements and architecture through deployment and production debugging.

Open to engineering roles and focused consulting work in robotics, AI systems, cyber, and deep tech. Based in Carpinteria and willing to relocate to LA’s South Bay or Orange County.
