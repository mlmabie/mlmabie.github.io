Software research engineer: AI/ML, robotics, systems

I work at whatever level the problem actually lives, from kinematics, data contracts, and failure traces up to product, strategy, and the culture of the team building it, and I move between them as the evidence calls for.

I build run-data, verification, and agent infrastructure for robotic welding at Advanced Metal Research in Hawthorne: the shared run-data platform, typed weld and materials models, motion research, evaluation, and the operator controls that run the robot. I also build the software-factory and agent workflows the team uses to develop and verify that software. Planning results are bound to source revision, robot model, and calibration, so a result can be re-derived rather than trusted.

Before that I spent three years on defense AI at RAM Laboratories. I was principal investigator on T-UEBA, a $250k Army SBIR in tactical behavior analytics, a core contributor to RAM’s DARPA AI Cyber Challenge entry, and a named co-inventor on DL-Patcher, automated vulnerability repair with a patent pending.

I started at sixteen designing robotic tooling for medical-device manufacturing at Applied Medical, and studied cognitive science, machine learning, and neural computation at UC San Diego.

Looking for a full-time role or focused consulting with deep tech teams in Los Angeles, especially El Segundo and the South Bay: robotics, autonomy, defense, and aerospace. Based in Carpinteria, working hybrid in Hawthorne, and relocating to the South Bay.
