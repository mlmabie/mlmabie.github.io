Malachi Mabie

Software research engineer: AI/ML, robotics, systems

I work at whatever level the problem actually lives, from kinematics, data contracts, and failure traces up to product, strategy, and the culture of the team building it, and I move between them as the evidence calls for.

At Advanced Metal Research in Hawthorne I’m the learning and autonomy engineer on robotic welding. I built the team’s shared run-data platform, from the robot controller to queryable evidence, wrote its verification ladder from lint through weld-sample proofs, and built its agent software factory. Each run is bound to source revision, robot model, and calibration, so a result can be re-derived rather than trusted.

Before that I spent three years at RAM Laboratories, a defense SBIR research firm, becoming a senior member of technical staff. I was principal investigator on T-UEBA, a $250k Army SBIR in tactical behavior analytics, a named co-inventor on patent-pending automated vulnerability repair, and a core contributor to RAM’s DARPA AI Cyber Challenge entry.

Through Mabie Industries, my S-corp for startup work, I was Drone Institute’s sole engineer for six production services, and I’ve worked with Siethar Group, Hammies, and Truffle Computer Company.

I came up through robotics and manufacturing. FIRST Robotics led to Applied Medical at sixteen and five years of robotic tooling, fixtures, and automation for medical-device manufacturing that still run in production. I moved into ML and software to scale that work with tools, and studied cognitive science, machine learning, and neural computation at UC San Diego.

Looking for a full-time role or focused consulting with deep tech teams in Los Angeles, especially El Segundo and the South Bay: robotics, autonomy, defense, and aerospace. Based in Carpinteria, working hybrid in Hawthorne, and relocating to the South Bay.
