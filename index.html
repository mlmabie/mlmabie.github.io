About

I’m a research engineer working on robotics, model evaluation, and AI systems. At Advanced Metal Research, I work on robot motion planning, operator controls, and runtime integration.

Previously, I led research in tactical behavior analytics and worked on automated code repair at RAM Laboratories. I started in manufacturing engineering at Applied Medical and studied cognitive science, machine learning, and neural computation at UC San Diego.

I’m interested in how models represent uncertainty, how we test their decisions, and when those decisions are useful.
