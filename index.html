I started at sixteen designing robotic tooling for medical-device manufacturing at Applied Medical, and studied cognitive science, machine learning, and neural computation at UC San Diego.

Looking for a full-time role or focused consulting with deep tech teams in Los Angeles, especially El Segundo and the South Bay: robotics, autonomy, defense, and aerospace. Based in Carpinteria, working hybrid in Hawthorne, and relocating to the South Bay.
